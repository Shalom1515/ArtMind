# ArtMind

ArtMind is an online painting gallery for everyone who loves art. You can browse 60 paintings by 12 artists, look at them up close, read about the people who made them and move through ready made collections built around a mood, a colour or a subject.

You do not have to know the right words to find something. You can search in plain sentences like "calm landscapes with soft light", get suggestions based on the paintings you view and save, and upload a photo of a painting to learn its likely style. When you have a question about a painting, an artist or a technique, you can ask the art assistant.

With a free account you can save favourites and come back to them later, and download the details of any painting as a PDF or Word file.
